By **[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com)**. Source: [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/marketing](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/tree/main/marketing). Licence: Apache-2.0.

Role packs (optional) · Marketing

## What it does

Campaign plans, content drafts, email sequences, competitor briefs, SEO checks and performance reports, plus a brand review of anything before it goes out.

## When to use it

- Marketing is your job, or a big part of it.
- You want campaign planning and reporting as well as writing.

## Try it

```
Plan a spring campaign for our trade customers: goals, channels, timeline and first drafts.
```

## How to get it

**Claude app (chat and Cowork)**

- Works in Cowork and the Claude app. Open **Customize > Plugins**, find **marketing** from Anthropic and install it. If it isn't listed, choose **Add > Add marketplace**, enter `anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins`, then install **marketing**.

**Claude Code**

```
claude plugin marketplace add https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins.git
claude plugin install marketing@knowledge-work-plugins
```
