## What it does

Turns raw customer material into findings: interview notes, survey answers, reviews, support tickets. It pulls out the problems, wants and exact phrases customers use, so your copy and decisions use their words.

## When to use it

- You have a pile of reviews, survey results or call notes and want the patterns.
- You're about to write marketing copy and want to know how customers describe the problem.

## Try it

```
Here are 40 customer reviews. Use the customer-research skill: what do customers praise, what do they complain about, and what phrases do they repeat?
```
