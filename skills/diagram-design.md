## What it does

Draws clean, branded diagrams: flowcharts, process maps with swimlanes, org charts, timelines, Gantt charts, customer journeys, funnels, two-by-two grids and simple charts. It saves them as a web page or image you can drop into a document or deck.

## When to use it

- You need to show how a process works, who hands what to whom.
- You want an org chart or project timeline that looks finished, not hand-drawn.
- A plan is easier to explain as a picture than as paragraphs.

## Try it

```
Draw a swimlane diagram of our quote-to-invoice process: sales takes the enquiry, ops checks stock, finance raises the invoice ...
```

## Good to know

The first time you use it, it asks for your brand colours (you can give it your website address). Keep each diagram to a handful of boxes: it's built to leave things out rather than cram them in.

Part of the **Diagrams and visuals** set, with Canvas Design. Diagram Design for processes, org charts and timelines; Canvas Design for posters, flyers and one-page graphics.
