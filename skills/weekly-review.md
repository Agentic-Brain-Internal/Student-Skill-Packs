## What it does

Walks you through a weekly review based on David Allen's Getting Things Done: clear out loose notes and inboxes, check your task lists, calendar and the things you're waiting on, then look ahead. It ends with one clear next action and a health check on how much you've taken on.

## When to use it

- It's Friday afternoon and you want to close the week with nothing hanging over you.
- You've let your task list slide and want to get back on top of it.
- You feel you've said yes to too much and want to see what to drop or hand back.

## Try it

```
Run my weekly review. Here are my task list, this week's calendar and my notes: ...
```
